# SmartPlug V1 (SP-PLUG-V1) - Known issues

## No active known issues

The device has been on firmware v1.2.0 since 2026-01-15. No critical bugs have been filed against this SKU in the engineering tracker. Customers occasionally report wifi pairing issues but those are generally resolved by the standard pairing-mode reset (hold the power button for 10 seconds until the LED blinks white).

For general troubleshooting, see the public product documentation at the customer help center.
